The music of Open Historia

All four pieces are by Scott Buckley (https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/) and are
released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License
(CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
The files are the composer's own, unchanged.

passage-of-time.mp3
    "Passage Of Time" by Scott Buckley
    https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/passage-of-time/
    Plays on the menu, as far as its build, and from its build on while a
    game opens.

clear-skies.mp3
    "Clear Skies" by Scott Buckley
    https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/clear-skies/
    Plays on the menu, in turn with Passage Of Time, and from its own build
    on while a game opened under it loads.

legionnaire.mp3
    "Legionnaire (2022 Remaster)" by Scott Buckley
    https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/legionnaire/
    Plays in the war room and while a battlefield is shown.

vanguard.mp3
    "Vanguard" by Scott Buckley
    https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/vanguard/
    Plays in the war room and while a battlefield is shown.

The game's own licence (AGPL) does not cover these files; CC BY 4.0 does.
